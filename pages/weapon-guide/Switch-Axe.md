## Why the meme page?
Sorry, Switch Axe is just not a good weapon in Tri. And I'm saying this as someone who picked up Switch Axe to farm Qurupeco when I first played the game. The weapon is just in a weird place where it's like a budget version of a bunch of other weapons, and yet this versatility doesn't actually amount to anything good. I suppose it's a jack of all trades, master of none weapon, which is fine. Slow is smooth and smooth is something. But the sum of all Switch Axe's broken parts just doesn't add up meaningfully in this game.

I really like the overhead chop which I use to hack Qurupeco's head on turns.
> Wait, I could be landing Greatsword charges or Hammer golfswings instead.

I really like axe mode in general because the depleting phial is really annoying.
> Power Phial in sword mode deals 25% extra damage though.

I really like Sword Mode up down to hit Deviljho belly and roll everything with evasion.
> Lance does this faster, with more mobility, without needing a phial.

I can do the same thing in the fight vs Diablos.
> Lance has a shield. A SHIELD. To fast counter roars!

And the damage simply isn't there. There's a reason they improved the damage numbers in 3U...

It does look kinda cool, at least.
