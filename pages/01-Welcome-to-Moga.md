# Monster Hunter Tri Walkthrough
## Part 1 - Welcome to Moga

Welcome to the tutorial! Chief is going to send us on some tasks before we start taking quests from Guildie.

### Video guide
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lVaIXspLZ2A&list=PLJm1iEzW-kshQbjFixM73Tz7mIK0vncWk

### Checklist
- Collect 1500z from Chief
- - Buy a full set of Leather Armor from Outfitter
- Deliver raw meat to Junior at base camp wreckage

Pick your preferred weapon, get out there, and do what the game says! Pretty simple. Then return to Moga.

- Collect 1000z from Chief
- Get 30 resource points
- Complete Junior's request
You might buy some bug nets here. I like to catch spider webs from the bug spot in area 3 at night.

Then, go out and kill jaggis plus whatever else you feel like until you have 30 resource points. Collect from some bone piles while you're at it - Mystery Bones, Monster Bone S, everything helps.

- Collect 500z from Chief
- - Buy Iron Pickaxes
- Mine iron ore from area 4
- Complete Wyverian Artisan's request

We are *poor.* Every drop of Zenny helps! If you don't claim them now, you lose it forever! Okay, it's not a big deal if you miss out on it, honestly.

You might want to grab a torch as Chief suggests. It's available from the unlit torch as you leave Moga Village and enter the woods.

Don't forget to deposit stuff from your pouch into your supply box!

- Collect 3 Fishing Harpoon from Chief
- Poke a fish
- - Mine from area 12

The big fish in area 11 only need a stab from the fishing harpoon to leave a corpse for carving. Other things, like sharqs and catfish, need to be morted (hit until limping), then stabbed with the harpoon. If you try to kill it with only the harpoon, you will be very sad.

To use the harpoon, wheel to it in the item menu and press Y.

Also, whenever it is convenient, go mine from area 12 in D. Island. You won't regret it! Start collecting Machalite Ore - you are gonna need a lot!

Once you're done in area 12, you can use the newly unlocked "Return to Village" option in the menu to warp back home.

- Collect 1500z from Chief

Congrats, that's everything in the tutorial!
